Our story

A new UAE brand with a simple mission — make beauty shopping easy, honest and delightful.

Beauty shopping, the simple way

Souqly started with a simple observation: buying beauty tools online in the UAE shouldn't mean scrolling through thousands of random listings, worrying about quality, or paying before you trust the seller.

So we're building something different — a tightly curated collection of beauty & personal care tools, handpicked for quality, sold the way people actually like to shop: a quick WhatsApp message, friendly human help, and cash on delivery at your door.

  • ✓ Curated quality — every product is selected by hand, not bulk-listed.
  • ✓ Honest pricing — fair prices in AED, confirmed before you order.
  • ✓ Human service — real people on WhatsApp, in English, Urdu and Hindi.
  • ✓ UAE-wide delivery — from Dubai to Fujairah, we reach you.
